## 🧬 About Me

I am a **Ph.D. candidate in Medical Genetics** at the **Center for Medical Genetics & Xiangya Hospital, Central South University**, supervised by [Prof. Jinchen Li](https://faculty.csu.edu.cn/lijinchen/en/index.htm).

My research focuses on **tandem-repeat variation and the genetic architecture of neuropsychiatric disorders**. I work with family-based cohorts, whole-genome sequencing, and multi-omics data to investigate genotype–phenotype associations and their molecular mechanisms.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Tandem-repeat variation** — repeat-associated genetic variation, missing heritability, and pathogenic mechanisms in neuropsychiatric disorders.
- **Family-based genomics** — inheritance patterns and genotype–phenotype associations across families and populations.
- **Multi-omics genetics** — QTL mapping, causal fine-mapping, mediation analysis, and regulatory networks connecting genetic variation to molecular phenotypes.
